# Portfolio

Personal site: writing, photos, inspirations, and curated links.

Visual language: `.impeccable.md`. Landing (`/`) is the source of truth.

## Bookmarks

**Collection**:
A named group of bookmarks with its own route. The name is chosen by the curator, not derived from the web. The route slug is assigned once from that name and never changes.
_Avoid_: folder, category, tag, list, board

**Bookmark**:
A URL on the web, saved into one Collection. Its identity is the URL, not a title the curator invents. Title and image are a snapshot of that URL, captured before the page is served. The curator can fill a missing snapshot by hand.
_Avoid_: link, pin, item, card
