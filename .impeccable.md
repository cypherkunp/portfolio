# Design context

Personal site for Devvrat: writing, photos, inspirations, and curated links. The landing page (`/`) is the source of truth. New UI copies that room; it does not invent a second visual language.

## Users

People who already know Devvrat, or found him through writing, GitHub, or a referral. They are on a laptop or phone, skimming. The job is "get a feel for this person, then follow one interesting thread" (a post, an app, a collection). Bookmarks is a public reading list, not a productivity database.

## Brand personality

Craftsman, technical, a little theatrical. Voice is direct and specific. The site should feel handmade and slightly terminal, never corporate and never "AI startup landing page."

Emotional goal: quiet confidence. You landed in someone's workshop, not a product.

## Aesthetic direction

**Tone**: industrial / utilitarian with one sharp accent. Dark first. Blueprint-grid canvas. Geist Mono as the site face.

**Reference**: the live landing page. Nav, section titles, Posts rows, Apps rows, Cover hero, colophon.

**Anti-references**: Linear/Notion tables, SaaS card grids, glassmorphism, purple gradients, Inter/Roboto, italic-yellow display words as a heading system, uppercase tracked column labels, hero metrics.

Inspirations (quote pinboard) and Photos (dense image grid) are medium-specific exceptions. Text catalogs are not.

## Design principles

1. **Same room.** Shell, type, grid, and yellow underline are shared. A new route is new content in that room.
2. **Lists for catalogs.** Metadata left, name, optional aside, arrow on hover. See Posts and Apps on `/`.
3. **Yellow is punctuation.** Underlines and rare hover, not fill, not italic last-words, not paint.
4. **Match the medium.** Text → list. Photos → tight grid. Quotes → pinboard. Do not force one of those onto another.
5. **Restraint.** Atmosphere comes from the grid and type. Do not add extra textures, glows, or staggered "AI" reveals.

## Tokens

Defined in `apps/www/src/styles/globals.css`. Dark is the default (`ThemeProvider` `defaultTheme="dark"`).

| Role | Dark | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `#0a0a0a` + `.bg-app-dots` | 8px blueprint grid, fixed attachment |
| Foreground | near-white | headings, names |
| Muted | `neutral-400` / `neutral-500` | dates, abbrs, descriptions, nav idle |
| Accent | yellow (`decoration-yellow-500`, `text-yellow-400`, `text-secondary`) | underlines, external links in Connect |
| Shell | `siteShellClassName` | `max-w-4xl mx-auto px-6 sm:px-8` |

**Type**: `Geist_Mono` on `<html>` (`apps/www/src/lib/font.ts`). Ignore `--font-sans: Outfit` in CSS tokens; it is not what the site renders.

**Radius**: Cover uses `rounded-sm`. Avatars can be round. Do not introduce large rounded-2xl cards on catalog pages.

**Motion**: route view transitions (`www-route-vt`). Cover sparkles + beams on the hero only. List hover is underline-offset-8 and arrow opacity. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Layout chrome

| Surface | Layout | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/`, `/about`, `/posts` | `(main)/layout.tsx` | Header, page, Footer (Connect), Colophon |
| `/bookmarks`, `/photos`, `/music`, … | `ToolSubpageLayout` | Header, page, Colophon. No Connect footer. |

Always wrap app pages with `PageContainer` + `Section` when the page is a titled catalog (Photos, Bookmarks). `Section` titles are `h2 text-lg font-bold tracking-tight` wrapped in `UnderlineText` (yellow underline, offset 8).

Do not invent a second heading style (italic last word, `font-light text-4xl`, uppercase tracked labels).

## Catalog row

Copy `AppsBlock` / `PostsBlock`. Do not restyle it per feature.

```tsx
<Link href={href} className="group mb-4 flex items-center gap-2">
  <span className="w-8 shrink-0 text-sm text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-400">
    {meta}
  </span>
  <span className="group-hover:decoration-tertiary tracking-tight text-neutral-900 group-hover:underline group-hover:underline-offset-8 dark:text-neutral-100">
    {name}
  </span>
  <span className="hidden text-sm text-neutral-600 sm:inline dark:text-neutral-500">
    {aside}
  </span>
  <ArrowUpRight className="ml-auto size-4 shrink-0 text-neutral-600 opacity-0 transition-opacity group-hover:opacity-100 dark:text-neutral-400" />
</Link>
```

- `meta`: date, 2-letter abbr, count, hostname, or a `size-8` thumb
- `aside`: one muted clause; hide below `sm`
- whole row is the hit target
- external URLs: `target="_blank"` `rel="noopener noreferrer"` plus the arrow

## Nested routes

Keep the same shell. Use the shadcn `Breadcrumb` primitives (`BreadcrumbList` / `BreadcrumbItem` / `BreadcrumbLink` / `BreadcrumbPage` / `BreadcrumbSeparator`) via `Breadcrumbs`. Current page is `BreadcrumbPage`. Links use `BreadcrumbLink asChild` + Next `Link`. Do not restyle the primitive. Page title stays a separate `h2` with `UnderlineText`.

## Do not

- HTML tables for catalogs
- Card grids for links or posts
- Italic yellow last-word headlines (that flourish is Inspirations-only)
- Uppercase `tracking-[0.14em]` column headers
- `border-t border-neutral-900` as a fake table rule
- Hover-to-yellow text color; hover is underline
- New fonts on catalog pages
- Glass, gradient meshes, glow shadows
